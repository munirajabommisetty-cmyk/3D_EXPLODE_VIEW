## 6. How to make it

1. **Record the app** (one continuous take is best): follow the ④ actions with Snipping Tool (**Win + Shift + R**), with **microphone and system audio both on**. You can reuse the live take from the 3-minute recording.
2. **Generate the narrator clips** (section 4) with your AI voice tool, or record them yourself.
3. **Edit in Clipchamp:**
   - Place the app footage for ① to ⑥ as in section 3.
   - Put narrator clips 1–6 at their start times.
   - Keep **about 8 seconds of the real Timbre answer** in ④ with nothing over it.
   - Cut waiting time (Understanding…/Preparing…) and add the `(skipped ahead)` caption wherever you cut.
   - Add the captions from section 5.
4. **Export** 1080p and check the length is **60 seconds or less**.
5. **Final check:** watch it once, looking only for personal data, terminals, keys or notifications.

**Do not:** generate fake app footage, type the question and present it as spoken, or replace the app's Timbre answer with another voice.
